Payment Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

At Nodical Technologies, we believe in transparency and clarity regarding our financial transactions. This Payment Policy outlines our procedures for handling payments.

1. Payment Method

Nodical Technologies does not process any payments or accept credit/debit cards or any other form of online payment directly through our website.

All payments for our services must be made via bank transfer to our official business bank account. The bank details will be provided to you on our official invoices.

2. Invoicing and Payment

  • All services provided by Nodical Technologies will be accompanied by a valid invoice.
  • The invoice will include all necessary details, such as our bank account information, the total amount due, and the payment due date.
  • Payment is considered complete when the full invoiced amount is reflected in our bank account.

3. Queries

If you have any questions regarding your invoice or this Payment Policy, please contact us directly at: contactus@nodicaltechnologies.com
